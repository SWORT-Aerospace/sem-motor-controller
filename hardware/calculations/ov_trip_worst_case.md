## Acceptance criteria

1. Lowest trip above V_bat,max plus the regeneration margin (no nuisance trip at full charge).
2. Highest trip below the lowest stress rating (MOSFET V_DS, link capacitors, DRV8353 VM) minus the measured switching overshoot.
3. Lowest release above V_bat,max, so the comparator releases once the bus is back in its normal range (otherwise the break cannot be cleared at full charge: safe, but the controller stays unavailable).

Status: pending; missing V_bat_max_V, V_regen_margin_V, V_stress_max_V. Until the device terms and these limits exist, the trip is not verified: blocker B-OVTRIP.

## Power-up and loading, to check against the data sheets

* The logic rail comes up from the battery side, before the precharge, so the link may already be charged while +3V3 rises. Below the comparator's minimum supply its output is not specified; the MCU is in reset then and TIM1 is not configured, so the gate outputs are off whatever BRK2_N does. Until the TL431 regulates, its node follows +3V3 through R_bias; the trip is defined only once +3V3 and V_ref are both in range. The firmware arms the break input when it configures TIM1 (`pwm_init`), after the clock and supply are up.
* BRK2_N is loaded by R_h, the PC3 input and the test point; the released level and the trip use the leakage terms above. ADC sampling on VBUS_SENSE draws charge from the divider node through 1 k; C207 holds the node, and the size of that dip against the hysteresis is to be checked once C207 and the ADC sampling time are fixed.
* The comparator has no other supply decoupling requirement recorded here; follow its data sheet.
