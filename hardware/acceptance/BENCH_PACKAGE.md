# Bench package

Generated by `tools/bench_package.py --doc` from `hardware/acceptance/procedures.yaml`, the KiCad netlist and the consistency checks; do not edit. Build the package itself with `python3 tools/bench_package.py --images build-g4 --board <serial> --out build/bench_package` after building the images (`firmware/platform/stm32g4/FLASHING.md`, step 1); CI builds it on every push (artifact `bench-package`, board `UNASSIGNED`).

The procedure, limits and stop conditions are [`docs/BENCH_ACCEPTANCE.md`](../../docs/BENCH_ACCEPTANCE.md); this page lists what goes to the bench with them. No calibration is included: none is hardware-valid yet.

## Firmware images

| Image | Role | Stages | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `semmc_stm32g474.elf` | operational | S2, S3, S5, S6 | The controller image: start-up sequence, protection, CAN, bench readout (g_bench). Drives nothing until both device descriptions are verified, GATE_DRIVER_CONFIGURED is set and a hardware-valid calibration is loaded. |
| `semmc_stm32g474_pwmtest.elf` | PWM bench | S4 | Acceptance stage 4 only: TIM1 switches its six outputs at fixed duties with DRV_ENABLE held low. Never load it on a board whose bridge is powered (precharge resistor fitted or the bypass able to close). |
| `semmc_stm32g474_timing.elf` | timing marker | S7 | The operational image with the status-LED pin (PE14) high during the fast interrupt, for the scope measurements of stage 7; the LED does not blink. |

Each image prints `semmc stm32g474 ...` and its revision on the UART at reset; the PWM bench image says `BENCH PWM TEST` and the timing image `TIMING MARKER`. A revision ending in `-modified` is not acceptable for a recorded test. The package's `MANIFEST.json` holds each image's SHA-256.

## Why the PWM bench image cannot drive a gate

Stage 4 switches the six gate-signal pins. What keeps the MOSFET gates off, and how each part is established (netlist checks of `tools/hw_consistency.py`):

| Check | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| PR1 driver ENABLE has one driver: the AND gate | PASS | U401.ENABLE on DRV_EN_GATED: driven by [('U402', '4')]; MCU only on pin ['7'] (OUTPUT_ENABLE_FEEDBACK = pin 7); pull-down ['R403'] |
| PR2 AND inputs are the MCU enable and HW_PERMIT | PASS | U402 inputs: pin 1 MCU_DRV_EN, pin 2 HW_PERMIT |
| PR3 every enable defaults off (pull-down to GND) | PASS | MCU_DRV_EN: ['R402']; DRV_EN_GATED: ['R403']; HW_PERMIT: ['R1020']; PRECHG_EN: ['R105'] |
| BP1 the six PWM pins reach only the gate driver's inputs (and their pull-downs) | PASS | 6 PWM nets: MCU, U401, pull-downs to GND and test points only |
| BP2 every bridge MOSFET gate is reached only from a gate-driver output | PASS | 6 MOSFET gates, each through its series resistor to U401 only; no MCU path |
| BP3 with the precharge resistor not fitted and the bypass off, nothing charges the link that supplies the driver | PASS | U401 VM on VDC; parts from a battery net to VDC: ['Q101', 'R102'] (precharge resistor and bypass P-FET); the P-FET's source is on VBAT_SH, so its body diode blocks battery to link. Stage 2 to 5 setups leave the resistor unfitted and PRECHG_EN low, so the driver has no supply while the bench image switches its inputs |
| BP4 the bench image never commands DRV_ENABLE | PASS | DRV_ENABLE is written only as GATE_DRIVER_CONFIGURED && g_drv.enable_pin, both false in every image (tests/integration/test_target_firmware.py, test_target_image_keeps_the_gate_driver_and_meter_inhibited and test_bench_pwm_image_is_separate_and_marked); PR1 to PR3 keep ENABLE low in hardware |
| BP5 the gate driver keeps its gates low when disabled or unpowered, and tolerates driven inputs unpowered | OPEN | DRV8353 behaviour with ENABLE low (sleep and gate pull-downs) and the input ratings with VM absent are in its data sheet (SLVSDY6, B-DRV), not read; until then the protection in stage 4 rests on BP1 to BP4 and on measuring every gate at 0 V before and during the test (acceptance S4) |

So the stage 4 protection rests on circuit facts (the PWM pins reach only the driver; the gates reach only the driver; with the precharge resistor unfitted nothing supplies the driver; ENABLE has pull-downs and the AND gate) and on the firmware never raising DRV_ENABLE. What the driver itself does with ENABLE low or without supply is not established until its data sheet is read (BP5); stage 4 therefore measures every gate at 0 V before and while the image runs, and stops on any gate voltage.

## Equipment by stage

| Stage | Energised | Equipment |
|---|---|---|
| S0 Preconditions | no | - |
| S1 Unpowered inspection and continuity | no | Stereo microscope or loupe; Four-wire milliohm meter (shunts); Multimeter |
| S2 Current-limited logic-power start-up | yes | Bench supply with adjustable current limit; Multimeter; ST-LINK with OpenOCD 0.12 or later; 3.3 V USB-serial adapter |
| S3 Power rails and clock | yes | Multimeter; Oscilloscope with 20 MHz bandwidth limit and a frequency counter function |
| S4 PWM and break behaviour with propulsion disabled | yes | Oscilloscope, four channels, 100 MHz or more; 1 kohm resistor on a clip lead (to pull a test point to GND) |
| S5 Sensor calibration | yes | Multimeter; Reference thermometer; The vehicle's pedal, switches and hall-sensor motor (or their harness) |
| S6 Precharge and fault behaviour | yes | Bench supply able to reach the over-voltage trip, with current limit; Multimeter, oscilloscope |
| S7 Low-energy motor testing | yes | Current probe on a phase lead; Motor on a stand with the wheel free; Bench supply able to sink current, or a clamp sized for the rotor energy |
| S8 Controlled load testing | yes | Dynamometer or brake load; power analyser on the battery input; thermocouples on the MOSFETs and the motor |
| S9 Controller and telematics integration | yes | The telematics unit with its own supply; USB-CAN adapter; the host tools (host/logger, generated DBC) |
| S10 Vehicle testing after bench acceptance | yes | The vehicle, its emergency stop and dead-man, a closed area |
