## Decisions this list depends on

* **Pack maximum voltage** (vehicle team): fixes the TVS, the DC-DC, the fuse and the reverse diode (section Vehicle supply); until then the vehicle power connection is blocked.
* **Event country**: fixes the LTE bands and so the modem board (A7670E for Europe/Asia, a North American module otherwise); no A7672-family board is sold at a retail USD price.
* **Log rate and attempt length**: the 16 MiB NOR holds 138 min at the bench rates and 27 min at full rate with the fixture's controller broadcast, then sparse logging (docs/generated/telematics_storage.md); the real controller's rate decides whether a larger NOR or microSD (driver to write) is needed.
* **The controller interface** ([`CONTROLLER_INTERFACE_REQUEST.md`](CONTROLLER_INTERFACE_REQUEST.md)): connector, tap point and termination fix the CAN cable, the connector count and whether the transceiver's terminator stays off.
* **Wheel geometry**: fixes the Hall switch, the magnet count and the mounting.

## Spares rule

Spares where bring-up damage is likely or the item is a consumable: +1 CAN breakout, +2 fuses, +1 TVS and +1 Schottky (once selected), +1 TMP117, +1 NOR chip, +1 Hall switch and +2 magnets, +4 contacts of each kind and one housing of each. No spare NUCLEO, GNSS, IMU, modem or display (single units in stock; reorder if damaged).
