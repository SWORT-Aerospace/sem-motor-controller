# Bench and vehicle acceptance procedures

Generated by `tools/acceptance.py render` from `hardware/acceptance/procedures.yaml`; do not edit.

Staged acceptance of the motor-controller board, from an unpowered board to the vehicle. Each stage needs the
previous one passed and recorded. Energised stages are carried out by qualified team members under their lab's
safety procedure, with a second person present. Nothing in this repository performs or automates an energised
step; the tools only read the board and check the records.

Recording: `python3 tools/acceptance.py template --stage <S> --board <serial> --out bench/records/<serial>/<S>.yaml` writes a record to fill in; `python3 tools/acceptance.py check <record> --config <vehicle yaml>` checks it. `tools/bench_read.py` reads the controller's own values (`firmware/platform/stm32g4/FLASHING.md`). A limit marked pending is measured and recorded, not judged; it says what it waits for.

## Rules for every stage

- Two limits are stated and checked in every energised stage: the battery-input (DC) current, bounded by the bench supply's current limit, and the phase current, which the supply limit does NOT bound (at low speed the bridge can circulate a phase current many times the supply current, and the DC link can discharge far above it). Phase current is measured with a current probe on a phase lead, never inferred from the supply reading.
- DC-link stored energy is 0.5 C V^2. Before touching or moving a probe: supply off, measure V_DC with a meter and wait for the discharge. An open precharge bypass does not discharge the link.
- Regeneration is disabled in firmware, but the MOSFET body diodes rectify the motor's back-EMF into the link once the line-to-line peak back-EMF exceeds V_DC (rotor speed about V_DC / (sqrt(3) p lambda)). Never spin the rotor above that speed with the board connected to a supply that cannot sink current.
- A step that fails a limit marked stop stops the stage: power off, record, and do not continue until the cause is understood and the step passes.
- Every record names the board serial, the firmware image and its SHA-256, the configuration file and its calibration version, the instruments with serial numbers, the operator and the witness.

## S1 Unpowered inspection and continuity

Find assembly defects before any power is applied.

Equipment:

- Stereo microscope or loupe
- Four-wire milliohm meter (shunts)
- Multimeter

Setup:

- No supply connected. Precharge resistor not fitted on the first board, so the DC link and the gate driver stay unpowered in stages 2 to 5.

| Step | Action | Expected | Limit | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1.1 | Inspect against the assembly drawing - polarity of electrolytic capacitors, TVS, diodes and LEDs; orientation of every IC; solder bridges on the LQFP-100 and WQFN-40. | No defect. | observation | stop |
| S1.2 | Resistance between the battery terminals of J101 (VBAT_IN to PGND), both polarities. | No short. The reading rises while the input capacitors charge from the meter. | [ohm] pending: expected steady value from the fitted input parts in the release BOM | stop |
| S1.3 | Resistance +5V to GND, +3V3 to GND, +3V3A to GND. | No short on any rail. | [ohm] pending: expected values from the release BOM (regulator feedback and load resistors) | stop |
| S1.4 | Four-wire resistance of each phase shunt and of the bus shunt. | The BOM value within its tolerance. | [ohm] pending: shunt values and tolerances are UNRESOLVED (motor and battery current) |  |
| S1.5 | Confirm PGND and GND meet only at the net tie NT1201 (inspection of the layout and the assembled board). | One connection point. | observation |  |
| S1.6 | Fuse fitted and of the rating in the release BOM. | As the BOM. | observation |  |

Stop conditions:

- A short on any rail or on the battery input.
- A reversed polarised part.

Evidence to keep:

- Photographs of both sides; meter readings with instrument serial.

## S2 Current-limited logic-power start-up

The logic rails start from the battery input with the power stage unpowered, and the MCU is reachable.

Equipment:

- Bench supply with adjustable current limit
- Multimeter
- ST-LINK with OpenOCD 0.12 or later
- 3.3 V USB-serial adapter

Setup:

- Precharge resistor not fitted (DC link and gate driver unpowered).
- Supply into J101, voltage above the LM5164 start threshold set by the fitted UVLO divider and below the lowest input rating of the parts on VBAT_PROT; current limit at its lowest setting.

| Step | Action | Expected | Limit | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2.1 | Raise the current limit in small steps until the rails come up; record the limit at which they do and the idle input current. | Rails come up without the supply sitting at its limit. | [A] pending: logic current budget (consistency item PW2) from the fitted parts | stop |
| S2.2 | Read the device identifier over SWD - OpenOCD `mdw 0xE0042000`, bits 11..0. | 0x469 (STM32G47x/G48x). | [hex] = 0x469 (OpenOCD v0.12.0 src/flash/nor/stm32l4x.h DEVID_STM32G47_G48XX at DBGMCU_IDCODE 0xE0042000) | stop |
| S2.3 | With NRST held low, measure MCU_DRV_EN, DRV_EN_GATED, PRECHG_EN and the six PWM test points against GND. | All at 0 V (pull-downs, MCU pins high impedance). | [V] pending: input low thresholds of the 74LVC1G08, the DRV8353 and the bypass driver (datasheets) | stop |
| S2.4 | Flash the controller image (FLASHING.md steps 2 and 3) and capture the boot report on the UART. | Banner with the recorded revision; calibration: none in flash; pwm: sampling only; can: off; gate driver and battery meter reported as not in this revision. | observation |  |
| S2.5 | tools/bench_read.py --samples 5 --json: state, platform faults, inputs. | state FAULT_LATCHED (no calibration); platform faults include gate_driver_not_configured, battery_meter_not_read, pwm_not_configured, can_off; drv_en_gated not set. | observation |  |

Stop conditions:

- Input current at the limit without the rails starting.
- Any enable or gate-signal test point not at 0 V with NRST low.
- Smoke, smell, a hot part.

Evidence to keep:

- Supply settings and readings; OpenOCD log; UART capture; bench_read JSON.

## S3 Power rails and clock

The rails are inside the MCU's operating range and the clock tree runs at its design frequency.

Equipment:

- Multimeter
- Oscilloscope with 20 MHz bandwidth limit and a frequency counter function

Setup:

- As stage 2, controller image running.

| Step | Action | Expected | Limit | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S3.1 | +3V3 at the MCU decoupling capacitors. | About 3.3 V (TLV75533). | [V] >= 1.71, <= 3.6 (STM32G474 operating VDD, ST STM32_open_pin_data@7d1f1514; the regulator tolerance is in its datasheet (open)) | stop |
| S3.2 | +3V3A at VREF+. | About 3.3 V, within the same range. | [V] >= 1.71, <= 3.6 (as S3.1; VDDA and VREF+ limits are in DS12288 (open)) | stop |
| S3.3 | +5V at the CAN transceiver. | Set by the LM5164 feedback divider. | [V] pending: LM5164 VFB (datasheet) and TJA1051 VCC range (datasheet) |  |
| S3.4 | Ripple on +3V3A (oscilloscope, 20 MHz bandwidth limit). | Recorded for the current-sense error budget. | [mV] pending: ADC accuracy budget (hardware/calculations/current_sense_error_budget.md) needs the CSA data |  |
| S3.5 | Frequency at test point WDG_WDI. | 500 Hz (one edge per 1 kHz slow step, TIM6 from the 160 MHz clock). | [Hz] nominal 500; pending: tolerance of the clock source (DS12288 for HSI16, the crystal datasheet for HSE) | A reading not close to 500 Hz (for example 50 Hz or 5 kHz) means a clock-tree error; stop. |
| S3.6 | External watchdog - halt the core with the debugger (DRV_ENABLE is low in this image) and measure the time from the last WDG_WDI edge to WDG_RST_N going low. | The MAX6369 timeout of the strapped code; the MCU resets and HW_PERMIT is pulled low. | [s] >= 0.02 (longest gap between WDI edges in the firmware (20 ms during the start-up offset measurement, main.c measure_offsets); the strap's nominal value is in the MAX6369 datasheet (open)) | stop |
| S3.7 | Two bench_read samples a known time apart - fast_count increase per second. | The TIM1 sampling rate, 10 kHz without a calibration. | [Hz] nominal 10000; pending: clock source tolerance as S3.5 |  |

Stop conditions:

- A rail outside the MCU's operating range.
- WDI frequency far from 500 Hz.
- Watchdog timeout shorter than 20 ms.

Evidence to keep:

- Meter readings; oscilloscope screenshots of WDG_WDI, WDG_RST_N and the ripple.

## S4 PWM and break behaviour with propulsion disabled

The TIM1 outputs, dead time and hardware break paths behave as designed at the MCU pins, with the gate driver disabled and unpowered.

Equipment:

- Oscilloscope, four channels, 100 MHz or more
- 1 kohm resistor on a clip lead (to pull a test point to GND)

Setup:

- As stage 2 (precharge resistor not fitted; driver unpowered).
- Flash the bench image semmc_stm32g474_pwmtest (it switches the gate signals; DRV_ENABLE stays low). Never load it on a board whose bridge is powered.

| Step | Action | Expected | Limit | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S4.1 | UART banner and DRV_EN_GATED during the whole stage. | Banner "semmc stm32g474 BENCH PWM TEST"; DRV_EN_GATED at 0 V throughout. | observation | stop |
| S4.2 | Frequency at PWM_A_HI. | 10 kHz (160 MHz / (2 x 8000)). | [Hz] nominal 10000; pending: clock source tolerance as S3.5 |  |
| S4.3 | High-side pulse width on PWM_A_HI, PWM_B_HI, PWM_C_HI. | Duty 25 / 50 / 75 % of 100 us, each shortened by the dead time: about 24, 49 and 74 us. | [us] pending: the dead-time insertion rule is in RM0440 (open); record the three widths |  |
| S4.4 | Dead time on each phase, between one output falling and its complement rising (both edges). | 1.000 us programmed (DTG 0x90 at 160 MHz). | [us] >= 1 (dead time programmed by the bench image (mc_tim_dtg_encode, firmware/tests/test_io.c); never shorter) | stop |
| S4.5 | Trigger on both outputs of each phase high at the same time. | Never. | observation | stop |
| S4.6 | Pull test point DRV_NFAULT to GND through 1 kohm; measure from that edge to the last PWM output low. Release, then press fault reset. | All six outputs low; they stay low after release until fault reset; bench_read shows break_driver. | [ns] pending: TIM1 break path delay (RM0440, DS12288); digital filter 4 samples = 25 ns is the firmware's part | stop |
| S4.7 | Same with test point BRK2_N (over-voltage break, BKIN2). | As S4.6; bench_read shows break_overvolt. | [ns] pending: as S4.6 | stop |
| S4.8 | bench_read over at least 60 s: adc_wait_max_cycles (how long ADC2 and ADC3 finished after ADC1). | A few cycles if the three phase currents are sampled together; a larger value is the sampling skew caused by the ADC3 regular scan. | [cycles] pending: the acceptable skew follows from the current ripple and the amplifier settling (DRV8353 data sheet, RM0440 injected-versus-regular timing) |  |
| S4.9 | Same reading: slow_overruns and stack_unused_min_bytes. | No slow step longer than its 1 ms period; painted stack left unused. | [count] <= 0 (slow step period 1 ms (firmware/platform/stm32g4/src/timebase.c): an overrun is a missed slow step) | stop |

Stop conditions:

- DRV_EN_GATED not low.
- Dead time shorter than programmed, or any overlap of complementary outputs.
- A break input that does not stop all six outputs.

Evidence to keep:

- Oscilloscope screenshots of each measurement with the time base visible; bench_read JSON.

## S6 Precharge and fault behaviour

The DC link charges through the precharge path, the bypass closes only when the link is charged, and the protective paths act.

Equipment:

- Bench supply able to reach the over-voltage trip, with current limit
- Multimeter, oscilloscope

Setup:

- Precharge resistor fitted; bridge MOSFETs fitted; motor not connected; controller image with a hardware-valid calibration.
- Battery-input limit: supply current limit at the lowest value that charges the link. Phase current: none (driver disabled).

| Step | Action | Expected | Limit | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S6.1 | Step the supply on and record V_DC against time. | An RC rise with tau = R_precharge x C_link. | [s] pending: precharge resistor and link capacitance (UNRESOLVED) |  |
| S6.2 | Bus-voltage divider - V_DC (meter) and raw_vbus; ratio = V_DC / (counts x VREF / 4096); write inverter.vbus_divider_ratio. | The ratio of the fitted divider. | [ratio] pending: divider values (UNRESOLVED, from the over-voltage trip) |  |
| S6.3 | Battery-side voltage from the INA228 against a meter. | Agreement within the INA228's accuracy. | [V] pending: INA228 datasheet; blocked by B-INA |  |
| S6.4 | Bypass closure - record the link voltage and battery-side voltage at the moment PRECHG_EN rises, and the time from supply on. | Closure at power.precharge_complete_fraction x battery voltage, within power.precharge_timeout_s. | [fraction] >= power.precharge_complete_fraction (calibration); blocked by B-INA |  |
| S6.5 | Precharge fault - remove the battery-side reading or slow the charge so the timeout expires. | Bypass stays open; precharge state FAULT; fault reset clears it. | observation; blocked by B-INA |  |
| S6.6 | Over-voltage trip - raise the supply slowly until BRK2_N goes low; then lower until it releases. | Trip 0.8 % above 2.495 V x divider ratio; release 2.5 % below (hardware/review/DESIGN_REVIEW.md item 6). | [V] pending: divider values from V_bat,max (UNRESOLVED) | stop |
| S6.7 | Safety loop - with the loop energised, HW_PERMIT high; open the loop. | HW_PERMIT low; state DISARMED; DRV_EN_GATED low. | observation | stop |
| S6.8 | Discharge - supply off, record V_DC against time to a level the lab's safety rules permit to touch. | Exponential decay through the bleeder and divider. | [s] pending: bleeder value (UNRESOLVED) and the required discharge time (rules / hazard analysis) |  |

Stop conditions:

- The link does not charge, or charges faster than its RC predicts (bypass or resistor fault).
- The over-voltage break does not act.
- HW_PERMIT stays high with the loop open.

Evidence to keep:

- Oscilloscope captures of V_DC, PRECHG_EN and BRK2_N; bench_read JSON; the calibration file used.

## S7 Low-energy motor testing

First switching into the motor at low current - hall alignment, motor parameters and the current loop.

**Blocked by B-DRV**: TI DRV8353 datasheet (SLVSDY6x) not read: pin numbers, operating limits, SPI register map, IDRIVE, VDS trip levels and deglitch, CSA gain/offset/settling, input pull-downs, behaviour with ENABLE low.

Equipment:

- Current probe on a phase lead
- Motor on a stand with the wheel free
- Bench supply able to sink current, or a clamp sized for the rotor energy

Setup:

- Battery-input limit: supply current limit at limits.battery_current_limit_A or lower. Phase-current limit: limits.max_phase_current_A at a small fraction of the motor's continuous rating, verified with the current probe.

| Step | Action | Expected | Limit | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S7.1 | Hall table and offset - low-current alignment; write motor.hall_code_to_sector and motor.hall_angle_offset_rad. | A consistent table on repeated runs. | observation |  |
| S7.2 | Measure R_s, L_d, L_q and the flux linkage (docs/VALIDATION_PLAN.md stage 5); write them with provenance bench_measured. | The configuration validates in hardware mode. | observation |  |
| S7.3 | Current-loop step response at low current (current probe and ctrl_currents). | Tracks the reference without overshoot beyond the tuning target. | [A] <= limits.max_phase_current_A (calibrated phase-current limit) | stop |
| S7.4 | Phase-current gain against the current probe, per phase. | Residual gain error recorded for the error budget. | [percent] pending: current-sense error budget |  |
| S7.5 | Fault injection - hall disconnect, phase-sense loss, CAN unplug, brake, dead-man, emergency stop (the host fault list, docs/VALIDATION_PLAN.md section 3). | Each produces the state and torque response in hardware/protection/SHUTDOWN_TRUTH_TABLE.md. | observation | stop |
| S7.6 | bench_read after each run: fast_late_writes (compare writes after the counter peak). | None; fast_margin_min_ticks and fast_isr_max_cycles recorded as observed maxima. | [count] <= 0 (fast-step deadline: the last compare write before the counter peak (firmware/platform/stm32g4/src/pwm.h)) | stop |
| S7.7 | With the image semmc_stm32g474_timing: width and period of the marker on the status-LED pin (PE14, high during the fast interrupt), on the oscilloscope, over at least 10 s at each test speed. | One pulse per PWM period; the widest pulse recorded with its operating point. | [us] pending: the required margin below the half period is a review decision; observed maxima are not a worst-case execution time |  |

- Phase current above limits.max_phase_current_A on the probe.
- FET temperature above inverter.fet_temp_derate_start_degC.

Evidence to keep:

- Current-probe captures, logs (ctrl_currents, ctrl_status), the bench_measured configuration.
