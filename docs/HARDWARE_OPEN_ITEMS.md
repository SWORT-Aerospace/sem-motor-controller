# Hardware open items (telematics unit)

What the telematics hardware still needs decided or confirmed before it goes beyond the bench, what the software does
meanwhile, and what closes each item. The software is written so that an open item makes evidence UNSUPPORTED,
UNKNOWN or unverified, never a guessed value. Blocker ids refer to [`blockers.yaml`](blockers.yaml)
([`BLOCKERS.md`](BLOCKERS.md)). Nothing here has been bench-tested: no board has run the firmware (T-BENCH1).

| # | Item | Status | What the software does meanwhile | Closes when | Blocker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | **Pack configuration (cell count, 13S or other) and maximum voltage** | open: only 48 V nominal and 10 Ah are team-reported; an assumed 13S was removed in v0.7 | no voltage class, TVS or DC-DC is chosen from 48 V; `battery_max_V` is null and the vehicle power connection cannot be set to allowed (`tools/telematics_bom.py`) | battery and BMS datasheets give the maximum charge voltage, the cut-off voltage and the cell count | B-BAT-V |
| H2 | **Input TVS** | open: the one pair checked (SMBJ58A with a 75 V input module) is rejected, its 93.6 V clamp at 6.4 A is above the module's maximum input | rule enforced: stand-off above V_bat,max and clamp at the surge current below the DC-DC's absolute maximum (`hardware/telematics/bom.yaml`, `supply.checked_pairs`) | a TVS and DC-DC pair passing both conditions is recorded with its documents | T-VEH-PWR |
| H3 | **DC-DC (unit supply from the pack)** | open, follows H1 and H2; isolated preferred | the bench runs from USB only; the schema's power tree (`power:unit_supply` feeding 5 V, 3.3 V and the recorder) is the bench's | module chosen, input and output capacitors per its datasheet, electrical check E6 passed on the bench | T-VEH-PWR |
| H4 | **Temperature sensors: TMP117 versus DS18B20** | open, **not decided in software**: the firmware drives TMP117 on I2C (the bench parts); DS18B20 1-Wire has no driver and is an option outside every total | the analysis is sensor-agnostic: a TEMP record with a status (ok, CRC error, timeout, out of range, bus error) and a value, or none; the thermal relation D1 uses a PROVISIONAL rate limit only | the team chooses per placement (battery surface, motor case, ambient) with each sensor's mounting and time constant; a driver is written for any part chosen that has none | B-NTC (controller side), docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md |
| H5 | **Controller CAN format** | open: the controller belongs to another team; bus type, bit rate, identifiers, message definitions, rates and delays are not stated | raw frames are logged whatever they are; the semmc protocol is a SIMULATION FIXTURE; the DBC adapter maps a real DBC once given; a constant latency is not observable without a producer timestamp (EXP-04) | the controller team's DBC (or protocol document) and a recording, decoded with every essential signal valid and confirmed by them | T-CTRL-IF |

| H7 | **Hall sensor electrical characteristics** | open: part, supply voltage, output type (open collector assumed, pull-up at the unit), active level, magnet count per wheel, air gap | edges on TIM2 falling edges with a 2 us filter; no edge is UNKNOWN never 0 m/s; a held line never shows standstill; stuck or disconnected channels are suspect (INVALID); `wheel_active_high` is configurable | the part and its datasheet (output stage, supply range), the magnet count and placement from the vehicle team, bench steps B1 to B6 with the real sensor | B-DRIVETRAIN |
| H8 | **Wheel circumference, pulses per revolution, gear ratio, freewheel** | open (vehicle parameters) | wheel speed and distance are UNSUPPORTED without circumference and ppr; relations B2 and the CSIA boundary are INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE or UNSUPPORTED without gear ratio and freewheel | values with a photo of the tyre sidewall and sprockets (B-DRIVETRAIN); circumference by roll-out (EXP-01) | B-DRIVETRAIN |
| H9 | **External storage hardware** | partly decided: W25Q128JV NOR (16 MiB) on SPI1 in the images since v0.7; the attempt length and the real controller's frame rate decide whether it is enough | NOR used when its JEDEC id answers, else internal flash; sparse logging at the reserve; frames beyond the queue's own-record reserve counted (`can_not_logged`); recording durations from the firmware's code (`generated/telematics_storage.md`) | the chosen storage holds one attempt plus margin at the rate measured on the bench (larger NOR needs 4-byte addressing, microSD needs a driver) | T-STORAGE |

## Not open, for the record

* The motor controller's own protection and sensors are the controller team's. Nothing in this repository's
  telematics delivery replaces or overrides them; no hardware item here gives the unit a path to command them.
* 48 V nominal and 10 Ah stay team-reported values ([`BATTERY.md`](BATTERY.md)); the advisory remaining-energy figure
  is labelled NOMINAL for that reason (`host/pit/advisory.py`).
