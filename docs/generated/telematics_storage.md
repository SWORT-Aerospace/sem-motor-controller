# Recording duration of the telematics log

Generated by `tools/telematics_storage.py` from the firmware's own code in the host simulator; do not edit. Measured on the host model, not on the board: on the bench, the status line's `slots=` and `left=` show the real figures.

Full-rate logging until only the reserve (32 slots at most, 1/8 of a small storage) is left; then sparse logging: one wheel record per channel and the counters every 10 s, nothing else (counted as `log_thinned`), 250 s per slot. A partly filled block is written after 2 s, so at low record rates a slot holds 2 s.

**Step B7 (75 min across the 32-bit timer overflow at 71.6 min).** With the NOR fitted, the full-rate log lasts 138 min: the overflow is in the full-rate log. On internal flash alone the full-rate log ends after 7.6 min and sparse logging carries on to 141 min, so the overflow is still in the log, in records 10 s apart: a time extension error at the overflow would show as a 4295 s jump in a record's time or span. The console capture (`tov=` and `t=` every second) is evidence independent of either storage.
