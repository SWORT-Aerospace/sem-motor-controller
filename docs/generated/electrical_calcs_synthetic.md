Nothing here selects or approves a component. Generated by `tools/run_electrical_calcs.py`.

## 1. Design bus voltage

V_design = V_max_charged x (1 + margin) + measured ringing. With the synthetic 12S pack (50.4 V) and a 25 % placeholder margin: **63.0 V** before ringing. The margin must be replaced by measured switching overshoot (double-pulse test) and any back-EMF/regen pumping. A device's absolute-maximum rating is not a continuous operating rating, and a 100 V-rated driver pin does not make a 100 V bus safe (the DRV8353 snippet data show VM 9-75 V operating, 80 V abs. max, VDRAIN 102 V abs. max - UNVERIFIED).

## 7. DC-link capacitor ripple and precharge

Ripple RMS (Kolar-Round form, verified numerically in tests/simulation/test_models.py) at 40 A peak phase current: worst case **17.3 A rms** at M ~ 0.65. Continuous ripple rating should use the real drive-cycle RMS (unknown); peak events are short.

- C = 1000 uF, R_pre = 47 Ohm: tau 47 ms, 95 % in 141 ms, resistor pulse energy 1.27 J, initial peak 54 W; switching ripple bound 0.50 V pp at 40 A.
- C = 2200 uF, R_pre = 22 Ohm: tau 48 ms, 95 % in 145 ms, resistor pulse energy 2.79 J, initial peak 115 W; switching ripple bound 0.23 V pp at 40 A.

Under the (unverified) SEM rule that the e-stop physically isolates the battery with normally-open contacts, the controller sees a hard connect on every enable: precharge sizing and contactor/relay inrush rating are mandatory review items. Discharge of the DC link after isolation must be provided (bleeder or active) and its time constant documented.

### Which missing measurements dominate component selection

| selection decision | dominated by (missing measurement) | why |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET V_DS rating | max charged pack voltage; measured switching overshoot; back-EMF at max speed | sets V_design (section 1) |
| MOSFET RDS(on) vs Qg trade | drive-cycle phase-current distribution (time at light load vs peak) | ranking flips between light load and peak (section 2) |
| parallel count | peak phase current and its duration; thermal path | conduction vs gate/Coss trade (section 3) |
| PWM frequency | motor phase inductance; light-load fraction | ripple copper vs switching (section 4) |
| shunt value / CSA gain | peak phase current; amplifier offset & drift; cruise current | full scale vs offset error (section 6) |
| DC-link C and ripple rating | drive-cycle RMS phase current; modulation index range; battery/cable inductance | section 7 |
| precharge resistor | DC-link C; isolation-relay topology (rule-dependent) | pulse energy, inrush |
| fuse / conductors | BMS limits; battery short-circuit current; harness length | section 8 |
| heatsink / enclosure | loss profile over a run; enclosure ambient; airflow | section 8 |
