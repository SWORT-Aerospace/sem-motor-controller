# Optimisation study v0.2 - SYNTHETIC

> **SYNTHETIC.** Vehicle, motor, inverter, battery and track are invented placeholders. These numbers demonstrate the method; they say nothing about the real vehicle. Generated by `tools/run_optimization.py`. **All v0.1 savings figures are superseded** (section 7).

Actuators are separate: **coast** (gates off), **propulsion** (controller torque) and **driver braking** (friction brake; issued as guidance, never a controller command). Every strategy below runs on one forward simulator. The route, start (rest at 0 m) and terminal condition (finish line; final speed free, no credit for remaining kinetic energy) are identical for all of them, and so are the finish target (1000 s limit − 30 s reserve), the torque limit, slews, the acceleration bound, the auxiliary load and the driver model (reaction 1.0 s, release 0.7 s). Nothing brakes automatically. Overspeed above a corner limit, or a missed stop, makes a run INVALID.

## 2. Acceleration-clipping audit

v0.1 clipped the *acceleration* (`a = min(a, a_max)`) but charged the energy of the *full* torque. The fix limits the torque itself, so the force, torque, power and energy belong to the same operating point. Test: constant 2 N·m launch, 20 s.

| a_max | method | steps limited | battery J | tractive work J | ΔKE J | resistive J | residual J |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| synthetic_a_max_1.5 | v0.1_clip | 0 | 16461.6 | 11939.5 | 11177.0 | 762.5 | **-0.0** |
| synthetic_a_max_1.5 | v0.2_torque_limit | 0 | 16461.6 | 11939.5 | 11177.0 | 762.5 | **-0.0** |
| tight_a_max_0.3 | v0.1_clip | 400 | 8713.5 | 4775.2 | 1825.3 | 187.7 | **2762.3** |
| tight_a_max_0.3 | v0.2_torque_limit | 400 | 2897.9 | 2013.0 | 1825.3 | 187.7 | **0.0** |

With the synthetic `a_max` = 1.5 m/s² the limit never binds (2 N·m produces < 0.8 m/s²), so the v0.1 numbers were **not** distorted by clipping. With a binding limit, the v0.1 clip creates work that appears in neither kinetic energy nor losses (non-zero residual). The v0.2 limit has none.

**Torque-only on track A is physically infeasible.** Coasting from rest at 700 m (top of the 1 % descent) reaches 5.99 m/s at the R = 20 m corner, where the limit is 5.48 m/s. No torque schedule can fix that. Meeting that corner needs braking: the driver's friction brake in the first vehicle version, or regeneration later (deferred, ADR-0007). The DP reports this; it does not brake silently.

## 7. Superseded v0.1 figures

The v0.1 study (39.7 kJ DP, 46.2 kJ pulse-and-coast, 55.1 kJ constant speed, i.e. 28 % and 14 % 'savings') is **withdrawn**. Its DP exported braking segments as coast. Its simulator braked every strategy automatically to an envelope. Its power map omitted the motor friction power. Use only the v0.2 tables above, and only as a demonstration of method on synthetic models.
