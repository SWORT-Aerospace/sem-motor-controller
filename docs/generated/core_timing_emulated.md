How to read this:

- These are exploratory instruction counts. The time column divides the count by the clock frequency, as if every instruction took one cycle. That is neither a lower nor an upper bound without a validated processor model: the Cortex-M4 can fold an IT instruction into zero cycles, and the counts include instructions an IT block skips and a few of the harness's own, while flash wait states (4 at 160 MHz, partly hidden by the ART accelerator), branch refills, load-use stalls and FPU divide and square-root latencies make the real time longer. Turning counts into a bound needs the Cortex-M4 instruction timings (Arm DDI 0439) and the flash and bus timing of the device.
- The platform's own interrupt code (ADC result reads, hall pins, duty writes) is not included.
- Deadline (`firmware/platform/stm32g4/src/pwm.h`): the last compare write must complete before the counter peak, half a period after the valley trigger, minus the ADC sampling and conversion time and the interrupt entry. On the board, `g_bench.fast_margin_min_ticks` and `fast_late_writes` measure that margin directly, `fast_isr_max_cycles` the whole interrupt and `slow_overruns` the slow step (docs/BENCH_ACCEPTANCE.md, stage 4). Those are observed maxima, not a proven worst-case execution time.
