How to read this:

- The lower bound assumes one instruction per cycle, the most a Cortex-M4 issues. The real time is longer: flash wait states (4 at 160 MHz, partly hidden by the ART accelerator), branch refills, load-use stalls and FPU divide/square-root latencies are not modelled.
- Counts include instructions skipped by IT blocks (the core still issues them) and a few instructions of the harness's call around the measured function.
- The platform's own interrupt code (ADC result reads, hall pins, duty writes) is not included.
- The fast step must finish within half a PWM period (`firmware/platform/stm32g4/src/pwm.h`): 25 us at 20 kHz. The measured worst case comes from `g_bench.fast_max_cycles` on the board (docs/BENCH_ACCEPTANCE.md, stage 4).
